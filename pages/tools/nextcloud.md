# Nextcloud: Finding Client Info and Playbooks

> Draft for the ORTSOC Initial Orientation Website (Task 2.2.4, Tool Section).
> This page shows where things are and how to use them. It does not copy playbook contents, since detailed procedures stay in Nextcloud (charter out-of-scope items 2.2.1 and 2.2.2).

## What Nextcloud Is

Nextcloud is ORTSOC's internal document hub. You use it to:

- **Claim a client** at the start of your shift
- **Look up client information** you need for an investigation
- **Find and follow playbooks**, the step-by-step ORTSOC procedures for handling specific kinds of events

## 1. Open Collectives

After you log in, click **Collectives** in the top center of the toolbar. Collectives is where client information and ORTSOC procedures are organized.

## 2. Claim a Client

Claiming a client is the first item on the NSM daily checklist. It tells everyone which client you are monitoring this shift, so two people don't work the same alerts. Follow the claiming steps in Collectives, then go to Kibana and switch to that client's data view.

## 3. Find the Right Playbook

1. Open Collectives and go to the playbooks section.
2. Look for the playbook that matches what you are investigating, such as the type of alert or activity.
3. Read the whole playbook before acting, not just the first step.

If no playbook fits, ask a mentor before taking action.

## 4. Use a Playbook During an Investigation

- Follow the steps in order and don't skip escalation steps.
- In your RTIR ticket, **note which playbook you followed** and anything you did differently and why.
- If a step is unclear or out of date, tell a mentor. Creating, maintaining, and following playbooks is one of the skills NSM students are evaluated on, so suggesting improvements is part of the job.

## Rules

- **Client information stays in Nextcloud.** Never copy client details into Slack, personal notes, or anything outside ORTSOC systems.
- **Don't edit a playbook on your own.** Suggest changes through a mentor or Rotation Lead.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Starting alerts before claiming a client | Claim first, so no one else works the same client's alerts |
| Searching the wrong client in Kibana | Match your Kibana data view to the client you claimed |
| Acting without checking for a playbook | Check Collectives first; ask a mentor if nothing fits |
| Not mentioning the playbook in the ticket | Name the playbook so the next analyst can follow the same steps |
