# Cybersecurity Assessment Rotation

> Research draft for the ORTSOC Initial Orientation Website. Pending review by the Assessment Rotation Lead.

## What Assessment Does

In the Assessment rotation, you will learn and practice cybersecurity assessments that assist clients in improving the security posture of their organizations. Using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and the CIS Critical Security Controls as examples, students are introduced to commonly used cybersecurity assessment standards. Working with an ORTSOC client, student teams will select a framework appropriate to the client’s cybersecurity goals and conduct an assessment. The results of the assessment will be a written analysis with recommendations to be presented to the client in-person or virtually while addressing the client’s questions and concerns.

**What you will be evaluated on:**

- Monitor and evaluate a client's compliance with industry-standard cybersecurity frameworks  (ex: the Center for Internet Security CIS Controls, the National Institute for Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework, and NIST publication 800-53, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations).
- Perform security reviews, identify gaps in security architecture, and develop an improvement plan.
- Develop cybersecurity assessment reports.
- Develop project plans.
- Verify implementation of cybersecurity controls.
