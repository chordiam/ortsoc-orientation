# Client Services

## Who We Serve

ORTSOC's clients are **small to mid-size public (government), not-for-profit, and educational organizations** in the region. These are organizations that cannot afford their own cybersecurity practice or a commercial company to provide one. In the teaching hospital model, they are the "patients."

This is not a simulation. Client systems and data are real, so a mistake can hurt the organization and the people it supports.

## What We Do for Clients

Each rotation maps to a service that clients receive.

| Service | What the client gets | Rotation |
|---|---|---|
| **Network Security Monitoring and Incident Response** | Their network is watched for suspicious activity. Events and incidents are investigated, ticketed, and reported to the client when needed. | [Network Security Monitoring](rotations/nsm.md) |
| **Threat Intelligence** | Weekly vulnerability scan reports, a threat newsletter, and urgent notifications when a high severity event affects the consortium. | [Threat Intelligence](rotations/threat_intel.md) |
| **Security Engineering** | Sensors and supporting systems that let ORTSOC monitor the client network securely. | [Security Engineering](rotations/security_engineering.md) |
| **Security Assessments** | A written analysis of the client's security maturity or risk, with recommendations, presented to the client. | [Cybersecurity Assessment](rotations/assessment.md) |
| **Penetration Testing** | An authorized test of client systems during client-specified times, with a report on findings and fixes. | [Penetration Testing](rotations/pentest.md) |

## How Students Fit In

You will work as a Security Analyst under the guidance of faculty, staff, and mentors. You may have the chance to contact clients about potential security events, write reports they read, or present results to them. Make sure that you communicate professionally, and check with a mentor first if you are unsure what to say.

## Protecting Client Information

- Client information stays inside ORTSOC systems and is NEVER stored on personal devices.
- Client information is NEVER shared outside ORTSOC, or with other clients without the data owner's permission.
- NEVER post client names, security weaknesses, or negative experiences on social media.

See [Mission](mission.md) for the ethics behind these rules.
