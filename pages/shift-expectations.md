# Shift Expectations

> Numbers below come from FY26-27 Student Manual 

## The Basics

- About **11 hours per week** of clinical rotation shifts, plus class time.
- Shifts run **7 AM to 7 PM Pacific, Monday to Friday**. A typical shift is 2 to 4 hours, and no more than 8 hours in one day without faculty approval.
- You submit availability before each term. Faculty assign a schedule that works for most students.
- Schedule changes need faculty approval and must be requested **at least 1 week** ahead.
- You are not scheduled when campus is closed.

## Showing up

- Be at your workstation, computer on and ready, at your start time.
- Attendance is taken every shift and **counted by the minute**.
- Short breaks are fine. Excessive breaks will be addressed.
- Tardiness and absences affect your grade.

## Absences

- Email **ortsoc-mentors@oregonstate.edu** *before* your shift. Leaving early also needs approval before the shift.
- **Excused absences can be made up. Unexcused absences cannot.** You cannot bank time before an excused absence.
- Usually excused when communicated beforehand: job interviews, certification testing, illness or medical/family emergencies, scheduled medical appointments, and military or legal obligations.
- Routine tardiness counts as an unexcused absence.
- Schedule make-up hours before you show up, since the facility may be full or a mentor unavailable.
- Requests can be denied. Expect an answer within about 2 business days.

## Professional conduct

- Be courteous and respectful to peers, faculty, and clients. Stay calm during incidents.
- Use a professional avatar on public-facing sites like Slack and email, and professional wallpaper on ORTSOC workstations.
- Guests may visit the ORTSOC room but may not use ORTSOC devices. Make sure nothing confidential is visible first.
- No photos, video, or audio in ORTSOC without explicit faculty permission.
- Faculty may bring clients, vendors, or officials in unannounced. You represent the program.
