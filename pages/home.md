# Welcome to ORTSOC

This site is your starting point as a new ORTSOC student. It covers what ORTSOC is, who we serve, how rotations work, the core tools you will use, and what is expected on a shift.

> **Note:** This guide supplements official ORTSOC training. It does not replace your Canvas modules, playbooks, or what your mentors and Rotation Leads tell you.

## Golden Rules

- **Client data stays in ORTSOC systems.** Never copy client names, IPs, or details into personal notes or outside chats.
- **Ask early.** If you are unsure, ask a mentor before acting.
- **Write it down.** Tickets and hand-offs are how the next shift picks up your work.
